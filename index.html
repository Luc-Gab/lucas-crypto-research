04 / Selected research

Ten digital-asset case studies

“Opportunity” means a research question and catalyst set to monitor — not a recommendation or price prediction.

ETH

Ethereum

Smart-contract platform / settlement
Settlement DeFi L2
Test whether Ethereum settlement, applications and liquidity reinforce ETH demand through staking, gas, collateral and settlement roles.

Key variables

  • Issuance versus EIP-1559 burn.
  • L2 activity and Ethereum value capture.
  • Stablecoins, DeFi, fees and developers.

12–36 months / 3–5 years

Medium term L2 economics, settlement demand, staking and burn dynamics.
Long term Ethereum as neutral settlement/collateral infrastructure versus competitors.

Invalidation

Persistent loss of economically relevant activity or durable migration of value capture.

Primary source ↗
SOL

Solana

High-throughput Layer 1
L1 Staking Apps
Separate durable application/payment demand from cyclical activity while tracking validator economics and issuance.

Key variables

  • Fees, app revenue and retained users.
  • Staking, validators and reliability.
  • Inflation versus real demand.

12–36 months / 3–5 years

Medium term Stablecoins, payments, DeFi, consumer apps and revenue diversity.
Long term Durable high-throughput execution infrastructure.

Invalidation

Sustained reliability problems or competitor ecosystems capturing the same applications.

Primary source ↗
LINK

Chainlink

Oracle / interoperability infrastructure
Oracles CCIP Infrastructure
Measure whether oracle, interoperability and data services become essential infrastructure for on-chain finance and applications.

Key variables

  • Usage and value secured, not just headlines.
  • Data Feeds, CCIP, Data Streams and staking.
  • Competition and security.

12–36 months / 3–5 years

Medium term Adoption and recurring demand for data/interoperability.
Long term Critical middleware across chains and asset classes.

Invalidation

Competitors capture high-value use cases or material security failures.

Primary source ↗
AAVE

Aave

DeFi lending / liquidity
DeFi Lending GHO
Analyze whether lending demand, stablecoin activity and controlled credit risk can translate into durable protocol economics.

Key variables

  • Liquidity, borrowing, utilization, bad debt and liquidations.
  • GHO supply, collateral and treasury flows.
  • Competition in decentralized credit.

12–36 months / 3–5 years

Medium term Lending growth, GHO adoption and risk parameters.
Long term Persistent decentralized credit infrastructure.

Invalidation

Persistent bad debt, security failures or durable loss of lending demand.

UNI

Uniswap

DEX / automated market maker
DEX Liquidity Fees
Separate trading activity and LP economics from the evolving protocol-level fee/value-capture mechanism.

Key variables

  • DEX volume, liquidity, fees and market share.
  • Protocol-fee activation and UNI value capture.
  • DEX competition.

12–36 months / 3–5 years

Medium term Fee flows, protocol-fee utilization and market share.
Long term Core liquidity infrastructure across chains.

Invalidation

Arbitrum

Ethereum Layer 2 / scaling
L2 Governance Unlocks
Separate ecosystem growth from ARB value capture while tracking governance, supply releases and network economics.

Key variables

  • Users, stablecoins, TVL, fees and revenue.
  • Unlock schedule and circulating supply.
  • Sequencer, bridge and competition risk.

12–36 months / 3–5 years
