04 · Opportunity framework

ETH · Layer 1 / settlement

Ethereum

Ethereum's medium/long-term research opportunity is tied to whether it remains the dominant settlement and liquidity layer while L2s expand execution.

Snapshot · 22 Sep 2026$2,733—

Medium term · 12–36 months

12–36 months: monitor L2 settlement demand, stablecoin activity, DeFi liquidity, staking and net ETH supply dynamics.

Long term · 3–5 years

3–5 years: evaluate Ethereum as a settlement/security layer for rollups, tokenized assets and financial applications.

Metrics to monitor

  • Net issuance vs burn
  • L2 settlement activity
  • Stablecoin supply
  • Staking share
  • DeFi fees and revenue
